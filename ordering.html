Ordering

Every one made to order. Just say when.

  • 1

    Send a message

    Tell me the flavor, the size and the date you need it. Instagram, email or text all work.

  • 2

    Confirm the details

    I’ll come back with a price and hold your date. A 50% deposit books it in.

  • 3

    Pick up or delivered

    Collect it locally, or have it brought to you.

Sizes
6, 8, 9 and 10 inch.
Pricing
Depends on the flavor and the size — ask when you message and I’ll quote you.
How much notice
At least 72 hours, and a little more for some flavors. There are no set baking days, so any date beyond that works — book as far ahead as you like.
Deposit
50% at the time of ordering to reserve your date. Deposits are non-refundable.
Delivery
$20 within 15 miles, then $5 for every 5 miles beyond that.
Pickup
Local pickup can be arranged — we’ll sort out a time when you order.

Ready to order?

Send over the flavor, size and date, and I’ll take it from there.
