GALLERY PHOTOS

gallery.html shows whatever photos are in this folder, named photo-01.jpg,
photo-02.jpg, and so on. Cards with no photo hide themselves, so the page
stays presentable while you add them.

TO ADD PHOTOS
  1. Drop the images into ~/Desktop/reprise-photos (any names, any format —
     HEIC from an iPhone is fine).
  2. Run:  ./scripts/prep-photos.sh ~/Desktop/reprise-photos

That resizes everything to 1400px and files it here in sorted order. The
captions in gallery.html are matched to the photos by hand afterwards.

BEFORE ADDING THE BIRTHDAY CAKE PHOTO
It has another person visible in the background and a dark kitchen behind the
cake. Crop tightly to the cake first.
